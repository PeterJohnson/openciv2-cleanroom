Stock TrueType fonts: DejaVu 2.37
https://github.com/dejavu-fonts/dejavu-fonts/releases/tag/version_2_37

GUI builds embed unmodified DejaVu Sans, Serif and Sans Mono TTF faces.
cmake/Fonts.cmake verifies their hashes and appends the full upstream LICENSE
(Bitstream Vera/Arev notices) to the build's THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt. Distribute
that generated notice with the application. These are independent font inputs,
not the historical bitmap caches in openciv2-assets.
