OpenCiv2 dependencies

Versions and content hashes are pinned in Directory.Packages.props, project
packages.lock.json files and dependencies.json. Every publication includes a
verified inventory and full notices for its selected NuGet/runtime/native/font
inputs. See docs/packaging.md and packaging/notices/sources.json.

RandN.Pcg32 and RandN.Core 0.5.0 — MIT
https://randn.dev/
Full license: packaging/notices/RandN-LICENSE.txt

Avalonia 12.1.6 — MIT, with upstream third-party notices
https://github.com/AvaloniaUI/Avalonia
Full license and notices: packaging/notices/Avalonia-LICENSE.txt and
packaging/notices/Avalonia-NOTICE.md
The optional UI also includes SkiaSharp/HarfBuzz/ANGLE and platform integration
packages; publication collects their exact upstream archive notices.

Inter font — SIL Open Font License 1.1
Full license: packaging/notices/Inter-OFL.txt
Avalonia.Fonts.Inter packages the font; the font license is separate from the
wrapper's MIT license. Font attribution is recorded by the publication inventory.

MicroCom, Tmds.DBus.Protocol and Avalonia.BuildServices — MIT
Full license supplements and source pins: packaging/notices/

Lua 5.5.1 — MIT, optional automation runtime
https://www.lua.org/license.html
The verified source archive supplies the full notice copied into native and
browser outputs as LUA-NOTICE.txt. The interpreter and small native bridge
contain no gameplay implementation.

SQLite 3.54.1 — public domain, optional native analysis exporter
https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html
The pinned amalgamation is unmodified; dynamic extension loading is disabled.
Its notice and verified build receipt accompany SQLite-enabled packages.

.NET SDK/runtime — upstream license and third-party notices
Self-contained CLI and browser publications include notices from the exact
pinned runtime archives. Framework-dependent desktop publications record that
CoreCLR is supplied by the installed runtime, not bundled by that publication.

xUnit.net v3 4.0.3 — Apache-2.0, test-only
https://xunit.net/
Microsoft Testing Platform dependencies are confined to tests. The full Apache
license is available at packaging/notices/Apache-2.0.txt.

DejaVu 2.37 — designated graphics-content font input
The asset inventory records the exact font/license hashes; graphics-pack font
integration remains M2 work. It is not a dependency of the headless CLI.
